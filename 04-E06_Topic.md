## Activation rule

A candidate is activated only after its unit of comparison is fixed. Activation must identify, where applicable: the Nation or Nations; the governing instrument or instruments; the relevant time period; the federal, provincial, territorial, First Nation, self-government, treaty, or other Indigenous-law jurisdiction; the estate or succession mechanism; the underlying property or land-interest type; and the specific comparison or consequence to be studied. Until those elements are fixed, every topic remains CANDIDATE / UNACTIVATED and may be revised, merged, reclassified, or retired without being treated as a substantive legal conclusion.
